# FireLens 30-Second Demo

Use large burned-in captions and keep the final video at or below 30.0 seconds.

| Time | Screen action | Voiceover / caption |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 s | Raw timeline and raw mode | “MODIS and VIIRS see the same burning activity differently, breaking the historical record.” |
| 4–8 s | Toggle to harmonized activity | “FireLens converts both into comparable 5-kilometer active grid-cell days.” |
| 8–14 s | Calendar; click September 2020 | “The harmonized calendar instantly reveals unusual periods.” |
| 14–20 s | Priority Review and map | “September 2020 reached the 100th percentile—774 percent above its historical median.” |
| 20–26 s | Show forecast-vs-observed and December outlook | “Tested against 46 later months, the forecast averages 139 activity units of error.” |
| 26–30 s | Method and CSV buttons | “Every result exposes its evidence, uncertainty, and downloadable data.” |

## Checklist
